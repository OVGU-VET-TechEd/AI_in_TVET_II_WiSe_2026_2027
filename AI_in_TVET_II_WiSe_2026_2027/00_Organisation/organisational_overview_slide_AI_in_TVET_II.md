## Start: Introduction to AI & Prompting

Every seminar starts with an introduction to Artificial Intelligence, today as a **compact refresher** that goes further than AI in TVET I.

{{1}}
<section>

| Building block       | Content                                                                                       |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **How AI works**     | LLMs: tokens, weights, training data, context window; retrieval and grounding; agents         |
| **Prompting**        | Role · Task · Context · Format · Examples; prompt patterns for learning material (quizzes, case studies, diagrams, LiaScript syntax) |
| **Tool usage**       | Text and structure (ChatGPT, Claude) · research (Perplexity, Consensus) · images and diagrams · LiaScript generation workflows |
| **Limitations**      | Hallucinations, fabricated references, didactically weak output, bias, cultural blind spots    |
| **Ethics**           | Data protection, copyright and CC licensing of AI output, transparency, automation vs. human-centred pedagogy |

</section>

{{2}}
<section>

**Rules for this seminar**

- Document your AI workflow: tool, prompt, output, your revision.
- You are responsible for the didactic and factual quality of every AI-generated nugget.
- Declare AI use and licence your course openly (e.g. CC BY 4.0).

</section>

## Learning Objectives

Based on the UNESCO AI Competency Frameworks (Teachers & Students) and the ICT-CFT, this seminar aims to:

{{1}}
<section>

1. Establish a solid **AI vocabulary**: model, weights, data, algorithms, workflows, agents.
2. Identify **pedagogical trends** in AI use: personalisation, adaptive learning, inclusive practice.
3. Explore **AI tools for assessment**, feedback and competency-based evaluation.
4. Introduce the design of **AI-enhanced pedagogical workflows** for vocational contexts.
5. Enable the creation of **micro-credentials and OER self-learning courses** with LiaScript.
6. Foster **ethical reflection** on the balance between automation and human-centred pedagogy.

</section>
