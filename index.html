About the Seminar

This seminar builds on AI in TVET I and focuses on the pedagogical integration of AI in vocational education: teaching and learning processes, assessment and curriculum design.

What we cover

  • AI pedagogy models (AI-TPACK, AI-enhanced didactics), adaptive learning, personalisation
  • AI for automated and formative assessment and feedback
  • AI vocabulary and workflows: models, weights, data, algorithms, agents
  • Hands-on creation of AI-supported LiaScript self-learning modules
  • Open education: Creative Commons licensing, micro-credentials, self-learning nuggets

Your product

An AI-generated LiaScript self-learning course for one UNESCO competency, published as OER.

Learning Objectives

Learning Objectives

  1. Establish a solid AI vocabulary: model, weights, data, algorithms, workflows, agents.
  2. Identify pedagogical trends in AI use: personalisation, adaptive learning, inclusive practice.
  3. Explore AI tools for assessment, feedback and competency-based evaluation.
  4. Introduce the design of AI-enhanced pedagogical workflows for vocational contexts.
  5. Enable the creation of micro-credentials and OER self-learning courses with LiaScript.
  6. Foster ethical reflection on the balance between automation and human-centred pedagogy.

Learning Outcomes

By the end of the seminar, you will be able to …

  • use core AI terminology correctly when explaining AI-supported learning designs,
  • design an AI-enhanced pedagogical workflow for a TVET subject or training context,
  • generate self-learning material with AI (texts, quizzes, diagrams) and integrate it into a working LiaScript course,
  • align one learning objective across the progression levels Acquire – Deepen – Create in a competency matrix,
  • apply open licensing and justify the human role in your AI-supported design.

Artificial Intelligence in this Seminar

Every seminar starts with an introduction to Artificial Intelligence – here as a compact refresher that goes further than AI in TVET I.

Building blockContent
How AI worksLLMs: tokens, weights, training data, context window; retrieval and grounding; agents
PromptingRole · Task · Context · Format · Examples; prompt patterns for learning material (quizzes, case studies, diagrams, LiaScript syntax)
Tool usageText and structure (ChatGPT, Claude) · research (Perplexity, Consensus) · images and diagrams · LiaScript generation workflows
LimitationsHallucinations, fabricated references, didactically weak output, bias, cultural blind spots
EthicsData protection, copyright and CC licensing of AI output, transparency, automation vs. human-centred pedagogy
Rules for this seminar
  • Document your AI workflow: tool, prompt, output, your revision.
  • You are responsible for the didactic and factual quality of every AI-generated nugget.
  • Declare AI use and licence your course openly (e.g. CC BY 4.0).
